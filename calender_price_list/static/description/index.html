Add a pricelist rule in three steps

01 / Open the calendar

Go to Calender Price

In Sales or Point of Sale, open Products → Calender Price. Navigate to the month you want to plan.

02 / Define the rule

Select a calendar date

Click an empty date to open the creation form. Choose the pricelist and applicable products or categories, then enter the pricing details.

03 / Save the schedule

Confirm the validity period

Review the start date, end date and minimum quantity. Save the rule to display it in the calendar.

Setup and requirements

  1. Install Calendar Pricelist on Odoo 19. The module depends on Sales Management and Point of Sale.
  2. Enable Pricelists in Sales settings and ensure users have the appropriate application and pricelist permissions.
  3. Open Products → Calender Price from Sales or Point of Sale.
  4. Set start and end dates on rules you want to review as calendar periods.

Pricing behavior: Rules continue to use Odoo's standard pricing calculations. For POS, configure the relevant pricelists in your Point of Sale settings.

Access: Creating and editing rules requires the corresponding Odoo permissions. The module does not grant additional access rights.

Hosting: Requires an environment that supports custom addons, such as Odoo.sh or an on-premise installation.

Frequently asked questions

Can I add a rule without leaving the calendar?

Yes. Select an empty calendar date to open the full pricelist rule form and create the rule.

Why is a rule missing from the calendar?

Check its validity dates, the calendar's displayed period and any active search filters. Rules without a start date cannot be placed on the calendar.

Are the Sales and Point of Sale calendars separate?

Both menus open the same action for pricelist rules. The records shown depend on your access permissions and search filters.

Does this add a screen to the POS checkout?

The calendar is available in the Point of Sale backend, under Products. It manages standard pricelist rules.
